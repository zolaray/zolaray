## 🔬 Featured Projects

### 🏥 MedGuard-XAI — Explainable AI for Clinical Risk Assessment
A 3-agent LLM pipeline (LangChain) that reads clinical reports and generates structured, transparent risk assessments for clinicians. Includes a dashboard with risk gauge, radar chart across 6 clinical dimensions, and XAI narrative explanations.

- 🚀 **Live demo:** [huggingface.co/spaces/zolaray/medguard-xai](https://huggingface.co/spaces/zolaray/medguard-xai)
- 📄 Manuscript in preparation: *MedGuard-XAI: From Urban Graph Intelligence to Clinical Decision Support*

### 🚦 UrbanGuard-XAI & T-GCN — Spatiotemporal Traffic Prediction
Benchmarked LSTM vs. T-GCN in PyTorch for urban traffic prediction on the SZ-taxi dataset, modelling road networks as attributed graphs using OSMnx. Extended with an LLM agent layer (UrbanGuard-XAI) that generates natural language explanations for GNN predictions — making black-box forecasts auditable.

- 📦 Repo: [Urban-Logistics-Shenzhen](https://github.com/zolaray/Urban-Logistics-Shenzhen) · [UrbanGuard-XAI](https://github.com/zolaray/UrbanGuard-)
